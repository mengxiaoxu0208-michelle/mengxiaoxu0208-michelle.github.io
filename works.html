Academic profile

Mengxiao Xu

Filmmaker, educator and researcher.

My work connects documentary practice, visual storytelling and media research. I am interested in the ways films tell lived experiences, and how cultural contexts shape what we see on screen.

Teaching & practice

I taught in the Film and Television Cinematography and Production programme at Hubei Business College from 2020 to 2025, and was appointed Lecturer in 2023. My teaching brings story development, production practice and critical reflection into conversation.

Alongside university teaching, dogmama lab has provided a setting for small-group filmmaking mentorship, with attention to student authorship and feedback throughout the creative process.

Student production & supervision

Guanbu A Week

Student feature · Co-supervising producer

Trailer · 1 min 52 sec

Guanbu A Week (《之后的一周》) is a student-produced feature film. The film received a FIRST FRAME Special Mention at the 16th FIRST Film Festival in 2022.

My contribution

As a co-supervising producer, I supported the student team across script development, filming and post-production. The production and its creative authorship belonged to the students.

Mentorship across the process

  • Script development and story discussion
  • Support during filming and production
  • Feedback through editing and post-production
